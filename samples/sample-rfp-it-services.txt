SECTION I — INTRODUCTION AND OBJECTIVES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CMS seeks a qualified vendor to design, implement, and operate a secure
cloud-based case management platform for citizen service requests across
twelve departments. The solution must integrate with our existing Microsoft
365 tenant and support hybrid operations during an 18-month migration.

The vendor shall demonstrate prior delivery of comparable public-sector
deployments within North America within the last five (5) years.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION II — SCOPE OF WORK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 Functional Requirements

FR-001 (Mandatory): The system shall provide role-based access control (RBAC)
with least-privilege defaults and quarterly access certification workflows.

FR-002 (Mandatory): The platform must support digital intake channels: web
portal, mobile-responsive UI, and API for partner agencies. All channels
shall write to a single canonical case record.

FR-003 (Mandatory): The vendor shall deliver configurable workflow engines
for at least three (3) parallel case types: Permitting, Code Enforcement,
and Public Works. Workflows must support SLAs, escalations, and audit trails.

FR-004 (Desirable): Integration with GIS for location-linked cases is highly
valued but not mandatory for initial go-live.

FR-005 (Mandatory): The solution shall export case data in bulk to CSV and
JSON, and shall support incremental nightly extracts to CMS-owned storage.

2.2 Non-Functional Requirements

NFR-001 (Mandatory): The system shall maintain 99.9% monthly availability for
production workloads, excluding scheduled maintenance windows not exceeding
four (4) hours per month with at least fourteen (14) days’ notice.

NFR-002 (Mandatory): All data at rest and in transit shall use industry-standard
encryption (AES-256 or equivalent; TLS 1.2 minimum, TLS 1.3 preferred).

NFR-003 (Desirable): Response time for common portal actions (search, open case,
add note) should be under two (2) seconds at the 95th percentile under nominal load.

2.3 Constraints and Compliance

C-001 (Mandatory): The vendor must comply with applicable privacy regulations
including PIPEDA alignment for citizen data and CMS records retention policy
(minimum seven (7) years for closed cases).

C-002 (Mandatory): Subcontractors handling personal information must be
disclosed in the proposal and bound by equivalent contractual protections.

SECTION III — EVALUATION AND AWARD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Proposals will be scored on the following weighted criteria:

  • Technical approach and architecture .............. 35%
  • Implementation plan and milestones ............. 25%
  • Past performance and references ................ 20%
  • Total cost of ownership (TCO) .................. 20%

CMS reserves the right to award to the proposal offering the best overall
value. Oral clarifications may be requested; all material commitments must
appear in the written proposal.
